# Daily brief

Use this skill to give the user a clear start to the day.

## Steps
1. Choose the single most important outcome for today.
2. Pick up to three supporting tasks, ordered by deadline and then by effort.
3. Note fixed commitments with times.
4. Flag one risk, such as a clash or a deadline with no time set aside.

## Output
Use these four labelled lines:
- `Focus:` one outcome
- `Tasks:` up to three items
- `Fixed:` times and commitments
- `Watch:` one risk, or `nothing notable`

## Rules
- Keep the brief under 80 words.
- Respect stated preferences, such as preferred hours for deep work.
- Do not give medical advice about routines such as sleep or exercise.
