# Meeting notes

Use this skill to make rough notes useful to someone who missed the meeting.

## Steps
1. Identify the purpose of the meeting in one line.
2. Separate decisions from discussion. A decision is something the group agreed.
3. List actions with owners and dates.
4. List open questions that still need an answer.

## Output
Use these headings in this order: `Purpose`, `Decisions`, `Actions`, `Open questions`. Write `None` under an empty heading.

## Rules
- Keep every line short and factual.
- Attribute a decision to a person only if the notes do.
- Do not add anything that is not in the notes.
- Leave out small talk.
